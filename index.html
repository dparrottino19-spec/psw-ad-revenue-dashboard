Closed revenue, revenue potential and purchases come from Searchlight, joined to Meta ads on utm_content and campaign id. Purchases are Searchlight paying customers, meaning closed jobs from the CRM, and will not match the purchase columns in Ads Manager.

Both ROAS columns divide by the Meta spend shown in the same row, so the arithmetic is checkable on the page. This will not tie exactly to Searchlight's own ROAS, which allocates spend internally.
